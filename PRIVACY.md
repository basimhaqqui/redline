# Privacy Policy for Redline

**Last updated:** March 28, 2026

## Overview

Redline is a browser extension that converts YouTube and Twitter/X videos to audio and stores them in a local music library. Your privacy is important — Redline does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

## Data Storage

All data is stored **locally in your browser**:

- Audio files and track metadata (title, artist, album, artwork)
- Playlists, favorites, and playback history
- User preferences (theme, Cobalt instance URL)

No data is sent to any server owned or operated by Redline.

## Third-Party Services

Redline communicates with the following third-party services only when you initiate an action:

- **Cobalt API** — Video URLs are sent to Cobalt instances to convert video to audio. No personal data is transmitted.
- **Genius API** — Track names are sent to look up metadata (artist, album, lyrics). No personal data is transmitted.
- **LRCLIB** — Track names are sent to look up synced lyrics. No personal data is transmitted.
- **Google Drive** (optional) — If you choose to enable cloud sync, your library data is uploaded to your personal Google Drive using your Google account. Redline only accesses the files it creates and does not read any other files in your Drive.

## Data Collection

Redline does **not** collect:

- Personal information (name, email, address)
- Browsing history
- Analytics or usage data
- Cookies or tracking identifiers

## Permissions

- **activeTab** — Detects video URLs on the current tab
- **storage** — Stores settings and library data locally
- **identity** — Authenticates with Google for optional Drive sync
- **contextMenus** — Provides right-click menu options in the dashboard
- **Host permissions** — Accesses YouTube, Twitter/X for content scripts, and various APIs for audio conversion and metadata lookup
